[Other Stuff](https://otherstuff.ai) is a Product Studio. We work with SME business leaders to put AI agents to work inside their business, and we develop and release products of our own.

Our products include Wingman, an orchestration environment for putting agents to work effectively alongside a business’s existing people, processes, and tools.
