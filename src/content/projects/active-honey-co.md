The Active Honey Co celebrates the unadulterated raw power, exquisite flavours, and healing force of Australian active honey. We’re on a mission to introduce the extensive range of health and wellness benefits of pure, wholesome, unadulterated Australian active honey to the world and inspire healthier, happier, and active communities.

Wild-crafted deep within our ancient old-growth forests in Western Australia.

100% natural, unprocessed, and cold-filtered—straight from beehive to jar.
