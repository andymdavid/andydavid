Normie Mode helps normal people understand what AI can actually do, which models are best for particular work, and why. Most AI evaluation is communicated through benchmark names and abstract scores that mean very little to someone trying to analyse a spreadsheet, review a contract, or research competitors. Normie Mode translates that evidence into plain-English comparisons—closer to Consumer Reports than an AI leaderboard—with the benchmarks kept underneath as evidence rather than forming the interface.
